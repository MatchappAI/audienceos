# The Crumb Report — Issue 42 (DEMO — synthetic newsletter)

Hi bakers,

This month my inbox has been full of the same three questions. First, people tell me their starter rises for a few days and then collapses, and they don't know whether to keep feeding it or start over. Second, a lot of you work full-time and say you simply can't find a sourdough schedule that fits a 9-to-5. Third, many of you are frustrated that every recipe uses different units, so you spend half your bake day converting cups to grams.

One reader wrote: "I have bought two books and a course and I still don't understand when bulk fermentation is done." Another said: "I would pay for someone to just tell me exactly what to do each day for the first month."

I'm thinking about what I could make to help. Hit reply and tell me what you'd find most useful.

Happy baking,
